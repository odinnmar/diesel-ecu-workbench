Customer-safe interpretation

READ THIS

With no same-engine dyno calibration, this tool cannot honestly know actual tuned HP from maximum IQ and boost-request maps alone. The uncalibrated result is only the power level implied by a simple proportional-fuel assumption. The sensitivity band is user-selected arithmetic, not a probability range.

For a defensible customer claim, use before/after dyno measurements or a calibration model validated on multiple comparable dyno runs. Confirm actual IQ, MAF, boost, rail pressure, injection duration/timing, smoke limitation and EGT in logs.
